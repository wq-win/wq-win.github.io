学习目标

  • 独立完成本学期及前置知识的陌生语篇读写任务。
  • 为答案写出可核对的语法理由或原文证据，并识别范围限制。
  • 依照分项量表评分，使用补救链接订正后再以陌生材料复测。

核心讲解

本卷20个编号任务，每题5分，合计100分。第1—16题为基础区80分，至少64分并订正错因视为达到基础训练门槛；第17—20题为迁移区20分。冲刺目标为全卷95分以上，且能够解释证据、在另一份陌生材料中复现表现。它们不是中考成绩保证，也不能换算为含听力整卷成绩。

先独立完成再展开答案。非写作题除答案外均写一句理由或原文依据，理由已包含在5分内；开放题按内容、语言和连贯量表计分，示例不唯一。建议给自己一个与篇幅相称的训练时段，记录实际用时，再依据错因调整；本卷不规定官方考试时长。不要预读参考答案来制造分数。

例题解析

仅示范评分方法，不属于本卷20题：短文说“Among ten students, only two stayed”。问“是否所有学生留下？”答“不是，因为only two仅指两人”同时给出判断和范围证据；若只写“不是”，判断分可得，证据分未完成。开放写作先核对要点，再看句子与组织，不要求与范文逐字相同。

练习

材料一 完形短文（第5—8题）

Our class started a book discussion group. The first book was about a young traveller who learned to respect different ways of life. At first, we tried to finish every chapter as quickly as possible. (5), our teacher asked what evidence supported our opinions. We realised that speed alone was not enough. Each member chose a paragraph (6) mattered to them. The paragraphs (7) on a classroom board, and everyone read them silently and wrote comments. We learned to disagree politely and explain (8) we understood a character’s choice. 本材料所有阅读与评论均以书面完成。

材料二 原创社区提示（第9—12题）

Community Hall Update Because a strong storm is expected on Saturday, the outdoor history walk has been postponed. The indoor photo display will remain open from 10:00 to 12:00 unless the building manager announces otherwise. Visitors should check the latest notice before travelling. Children under twelve must stay with an adult. Do not enter areas marked “Closed”. Questions about access can be sent to the organiser. 本提示为语言练习的虚构信息。真实灾害情境以当地应急部门及现场管理通知为准。

材料三 原创自然观察文本（第13—16题）

A student group wanted to make a garden look perfectly neat. They removed some fallen branches, then noticed small insects hiding under the remaining wood. Their science teacher asked them to compare two corners: one that had been cleared and another that still had leaves and branches. During several short observations, the second corner contained more kinds of visible insects. The students did not claim that every untidy place was healthy. Instead, they decided to keep a small natural corner while removing rubbish from paths. A sign explained why the two tasks were different.

参考答案与解析

1. G8B-CHECK-E01 答案

The old map was repaired by volunteers last week. 单数过去被动was repaired3分，保留意义及说明2分。

2. G8B-CHECK-E02 答案

Could you tell me where the hall is? 从句陈述语序，修改3分，解释2分。

3. G8B-CHECK-E03 答案

that（which或省略关系词也合语法，但给定选项取that）。cleaned缺宾语，不是地点状语。答案3分，作用2分。

4. G8B-CHECK-E04 答案

The child is not old enough to enter alone. 反义old与not、enough结构3分，含义保持2分。

5. G8B-CHECK-E05 答案

C。快速读与老师要求证据形成转折，However。选择3分，逻辑2分。

6. G8B-CHECK-E06 答案

B。先行词paragraph，关系词作mattered主语。选择3分，作用2分。

A。paragraphs为被展示对象且复数，过去语境用were displayed。选择3分，数/语态2分。

延期户外history walk；不是所有活动取消，室内照片展有条件继续。对象2分，范围3分。

11岁不能，须成人；12岁不在under twelve范围内，但仍须遵守其他规则。两判断各2分，范围1分。

不能。展览开放不撤销Do not enter areas marked Closed的局部禁令。判断2分，范围解释3分。

13. G8B-CHECK-E13 答案

想让花园整齐；发现剩余木枝下藏昆虫，转向比较不同角落。目标2分，触发证据3分。

14. G8B-CHECK-E14 答案

在几次短观察中，保留枝叶的角落可见昆虫种类更多；范围是该观察，不是所有环境。结果3分，限制2分。

15. G8B-CHECK-E15 答案

保留部分栖息环境与移除人为垃圾/保持通道为不同任务，不能把自然枝叶与路径垃圾等同。两个目的各2分，区分1分。

16. G8B-CHECK-E16 答案

B。行动根据局部观察调整并明确未泛化；A/C扩大范围。选择2分，依据及排除3分。

17. G8B-CHECK-E17 答案

It has been postponed because a strong storm is expected./The notice says the outdoor walk has been postponed. 状态3分，依据与英语2分。

18. G8B-CHECK-E18 答案

No. It will be open unless the manager announces otherwise, so we should check the latest notice. 否定绝对开放1分，条件2分，核实行动2分。

19. G8B-CHECK-E19 答案

I see your point about keeping the paths clear, but some insects were hiding under the branches. We could keep a small natural corner and remove rubbish from the paths. 礼貌1分，证据2分，有边界建议2分。

20. G8B-CHECK-E20 答案

示例：The students first wanted to make the whole garden look neat. After removing some branches, they noticed insects under the remaining wood. Their teacher encouraged them to compare a cleared corner with one that still had leaves and branches. During several observations, the second corner showed more kinds of visible insects. The group then changed its plan. It kept one small natural area but continued to remove rubbish from paths. This decision was based on what the students observed, not on a rule that every untidy place is healthy. The sign helped other visitors understand the difference between useful shelter and unwanted litter. 内容2分，时态及复杂句2分，证据范围与连贯1分。
