# 主谓一致与it 把长句的主干找回来

七至九年级原创英语读写课程 · 8年级下学期 · 第6课

**适用与边界**：按初中知识依赖循序推进，不冒充学校教材的精确单元顺序。全部短文、情境、题目为自编，不含听力。文中的80与95是本课程练习目标，不是中考分数承诺。

<a id="objectives"></a>
## 学习目标

- 根据真正主语而非邻近名词选择谓语形式。
- 正确使用there be、形式主语it及常见it句型。
- 识别并改正一致、代词、名词数与非谓语混合错误。

<a id="core"></a>
## 核心讲解

### 找中心词 不追着最后一个名词跑
The colour of these bags is bright。主语中心是colour，of these bags为修饰；不能因bags近就用are。Everyone/each/each student通常用单数；each of the students也按单数。one of the boys is...；a number of students are...表示许多学生，the number of students is...表示学生数量。

两个主语由and连接一般复数，但若作为一个整体或同一对象须看意义。with/as well as等附加信息通常不改变中心主语的数：The teacher with two students is here。either...or和neither...nor连接主语时，常按靠近谓语的成分一致；为了清晰，写作可避免结构过度复杂。family/team作为整体常单数，强调成员时部分英语变体可用复数，应看语境和一致性，不能断言永远只有一种。

it可指前述同一物，也可指天气、时间、距离：It is rainy；It is six；It is two kilometres to school。形式主语It is useful to review让真正的动作主语to review后移。It takes sb time to do表示花时；It is+形容词+for sb+to do常评价事情对某人的难易；It is kind of you to help评价人的品性。

there be介绍存在，it不能替它引出“有”：There are three books on the desk。there be中的主谓按存在对象，疑问回答用there。纠错按主干→时态/语态→从句→小词的顺序，别先改最显眼但原本正确的介词。

<a id="theme-extension"></a>
### 主题阅读与词汇扩展 跨文化交流与不带偏见的比较

以下为同课主题拓展的已讲词条，每条包含本课释义、搭配和原创例句。可遮中文回忆，再改写一个例句；不是要求一次背完，也不冒充官方完整词表。

|词或表达|本课意思|搭配|原创例句|
|---|---|---|---|
|etiquette|礼仪|local etiquette|The visitor asks about local etiquette.|
|tradition|传统|a family tradition|This tradition is important to one family.|
|similarity|相似处|find a similarity|The students find a similarity between two stories.|
|diversity|多样性|cultural diversity|The display presents cultural diversity without ranking people.|
|stereotype|刻板印象|avoid stereotypes|We should avoid stereotypes about a whole country.|
|individual|个人 个体的|individual choices|Individual choices may differ within one community.|
|background|背景|a cultural background|A person's background does not tell us every preference.|
|occasion|场合|a special occasion|The meal marks a special occasion.|
|respectfully|尊重地|ask respectfully|Visitors can ask respectfully when they are unsure.|
|misread|误读|misread a gesture|A character misreads a gesture and then asks for clarification.|

**选读脚手架**：这段主题阅读用于在语境中复现词汇，不要求初次学习就独立认出所有词。先读后面的中文问题，再找谁、做什么、为什么；未列入词表的难词可查阅或请教师解释，不因出现过一次就计作已掌握。

**原创主题短文**

Two students compare how their families welcome guests. One family usually offers a large meal; the other often begins with a walk around the neighbourhood. Neither student treats the other's account as a rule for an entire country. They ask what the activity means to that particular family and whether it changes with the occasion. Their report uses “in this family” and “sometimes” rather than “everyone always”. Careful grammar helps them keep the comparison fair.

**读写示范问题**：为何in this family与sometimes比everyone always更准确？

**证据解析与迁移**：资料仅是两家经验，活动还随场合变化，范围词可防止刻板概括。主谓一致也要正确：Everyone has...，Individuals have...；语法准确与文化尊重共同服务精确表达。

以上是有解析的教学示范，不另计入独立编号练习；计分任务总数见练习区。

<a id="worked"></a>
## 例题解析

**例1**：The list of new words ___ long。真正主语list，选is，words不是主语中心。

**例2**：A number of visitors ___ here. The number of visitors ___ rising。前空are表许多访客，后空is表数量。

**例3**：It is difficult for me to carry the box评价任务难度；It is kind of you to carry it for me评价you的善意，for/of不能盲换。

## 参考答案与解析

### 1. G8B06-E01 答案

is。中心词price单数，of these books修饰它。答案6分，主语分析4分。

### 2. G8B06-E02 答案

is。each of...按单数处理。答案6分，依据4分。

### 3. G8B06-E03 答案

are；is。许多学生与学生数量意义不同。每空5分。

### 4. G8B06-E04 答案

is。with附加信息不把teacher变成复数主语。答案6分，中心词4分。

### 5. G8B06-E05 答案

are。离谓语近的students复数；在本课所教neither...nor结构按就近一致。形式6分，依据4分。

### 6. G8B06-E06 答案

There；It。前句引存在，后句说天气。每空5分。

### 7. G8B06-E07 答案

of；for。前者评价人的品性，后者评价任务对某人的难度。每空5分。

### 8. G8B06-E08 答案

It takes me twenty minutes to walk to school. 人称变宾格me，time+to do。结构8分，说明2分。

### 9. G8B06-E09 答案

The number of books is growing. It has taken us a month to sort them. number中心单数用is；it配has。两处修改各3分，两理由各2分。

### 10. G8B06-E10 答案

示例：One of my useful habits is reviewing my word list every Friday. The number of examples in my notebook is growing, so it is necessary to keep them in order. I put grammar notes and reading notes in different sections. It takes me about ten minutes to check each section. I also mark questions that I still cannot explain. This helps me use my next study period well. A clear notebook does not replace practice, but it makes careful practice easier to organise. 内容4分，主谓及it结构4分，逻辑与限制2分。
