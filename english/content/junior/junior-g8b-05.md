# 状语从句进阶 让步条件和结果不混淆

七至九年级原创英语读写课程 · 8年级下学期 · 第5课

**适用与边界**：按初中知识依赖循序推进，不冒充学校教材的精确单元顺序。全部短文、情境、题目为自编，不含听力。文中的80与95是本课程练习目标，不是中考分数承诺。

<a id="objectives"></a>
## 学习目标

- 区别although、even though、unless及if等逻辑关系。
- 使用so/such...that与too...to、enough...to表达结果与程度。
- 识别unless、not...until等带否定的条件边界。

<a id="core"></a>
## 核心讲解

### 让步与原因不能互换
Although/though/even though表示即使存在某障碍，主句结果仍发生：Although it was cold, we continued。because说明结果的原因：We stayed inside because it was cold。even though通常强调已知或设定为事实的让步，even if常表示假设即使；初中写作先保证关系准确，不为复杂而强用。

unless约等于if...not：Unless you hurry, you will miss the bus≈If you don't hurry, you will miss the bus。不能再随意加not造成双重否定。未来条件从句通常一般现在时，主句可will/情态/祈使。as long as表示只要，条件要与主句一起读。

so+形容词/副词+that从句：The bag was so heavy that I couldn't carry it；such+(a/an)+形容词+名词+that：It was such a heavy bag that...。so many/much/few/little（数量义）+名词+that是常见特殊组合。so that可引目的：Speak clearly so that everyone can understand，不能见so与that就都判结果。

too+形容词+to do常含太...以至不能；形容词+enough+to do表示足够...可以：too young to join≈not old enough to join。若不定式动词已有前面主语作逻辑宾语，不重复it：The box is too heavy to carry，而非to carry it。不同主语用for sb：easy for me to understand。

### 主题阅读与词汇扩展 自然灾害信息与条件计划

以下为同课主题拓展的已讲词条，每条包含本课释义、搭配和原创例句。可遮中文回忆，再改写一个例句；不是要求一次背完，也不冒充官方完整词表。

|词或表达|本课意思|搭配|原创例句|
|---|---|---|---|
|flood|洪水|a flood warning|The fictional notice mentions a flood warning.|
|earthquake|地震|an earthquake drill|The school reviews its earthquake drill instructions.|
|drought|干旱|a period of drought|The article discusses a fictional period of drought.|
|warning|预警 警告|an official warning|Check the official warning for current information.|
|emergency|紧急情况|an emergency plan|The emergency plan is checked by responsible staff.|
|evacuate|疏散|evacuate a building|Follow authorised instructions if asked to evacuate.|
|route|路线|a safe route|The manager confirms which route is available.|
|authority|主管部门|local authorities|Local authorities provide current instructions.|
|supply|供给物资|emergency supplies|The lesson labels items in a fictional supply list.|
|update|更新|the latest update|Read the latest update rather than an old screenshot.|

**选读脚手架**：这段主题阅读用于在语境中复现词汇，不要求初次学习就独立认出所有词。先读后面的中文问题，再找谁、做什么、为什么；未列入词表的难词可查阅或请教师解释，不因出现过一次就计作已掌握。

**原创主题短文**

A fictional town plans an outdoor exhibition, but a weather warning changes the arrangements. The first notice says the event will move indoors if the hall is available. A later update says the hall must remain closed until an inspection is complete. Students compare the two notices and realise that the earlier indoor plan is no longer confirmed. They practise writing a clear message that states what is known and directs readers to the current official update.

**读写示范问题**：为什么不能仍按第一则通知去室内展览？until从句给什么限制？

**证据解析与迁移**：后续更新改变状态，必须等检查完成，早先有条件安排不是最终确认。真实灾害应遵循当地部门与现场负责人指令；本示例只练读懂条件和更新，不教替代应急流程。

以上是有解析的教学示范，不另计入独立编号练习；计分任务总数见练习区。

<a id="worked"></a>
## 例题解析

**例1**：___ he was tired, he finished the report。完成与疲惫构成让步，Although/Though可用；because会说疲惫导致完成，通常不合此语境。

**例2**：Unless it rains, we will meet outside。只要不下雨就室外见面，不能译成如果下雨就见面。

**例3**：The question is too difficult for me to answer → The question is so difficult that I can't answer it。不定式版本不再加it；完整从句中answer需要自己的宾语it。

## 练习

**作答要求**：除填写答案或写句子外，非写作题均补一句简短理由或原文依据；答案中说明/依据的分数正用于这一步。开放写作按所列量表评分，不必另写语法理由。

### 1. G8B05-E01

填although/because：___ it was raining, they continued the outdoor game.

### 2. G8B05-E02

### 3. G8B05-E03

填空：Unless she ___ (come), we will start without her.

### 4. G8B05-E04

填so/such：It was ___ an interesting story that I read it twice.

### 5. G8B05-E05

### 6. G8B05-E06

### 7. G8B05-E07

改错：The box is too heavy to carry it.

### 8. G8B05-E08

### 9. G8B05-E09

规则：You can enter the room as long as you have a visitor pass. Children under twelve must also be with an adult. 11岁孩子有pass能独自进吗？请保留两个条件。

<a id="answers"></a>
## 参考答案与解析

### 1. G8B05-E01 答案

Although。雨本可阻碍活动却继续，是让步。选择5分，关系5分。

### 2. G8B05-E02 答案

If you don’t study the map, you may get lost. unless转换为if not，保留结果。转换7分，否定解释3分。

### 3. G8B05-E03 答案

comes。unless未来条件从句用一般现在时，she第三人称。形式6分，依据4分。

### 4. G8B05-E04 答案

such。后有an+形容词+单数名词，用such an...that。形式6分，结构4分。

### 5. G8B05-E05 答案

so。quietly副词，so+副词+that。形式6分，结构4分。

### 6. G8B05-E06 答案

She is not old enough to join. too young对应not old enough，形容词需换反义。语义4分，结构4分，说明2分。

### 7. G8B05-E07 答案

The box is too heavy to carry. box已为carry的逻辑宾语，删重复it。修改6分，结构解释4分。

### 8. G8B05-E08 答案

目的，以便所有人能照步骤做；不是先描述一个程度再给结果的so...that。识别5分，区别5分。

### 9. G8B05-E09 答案

不能。有pass只是一个条件，under twelve还须成人陪同。判断3分，pass条件3分，年龄附加条件4分。不能将as long as一段脱离后文解释成无其他限制。

### 10. G8B05-E10 答案

示例：Although a long English article may look difficult, you do not have to understand every word at once. First, read the title and the opening sentences so that you can identify the topic. Then mark the words that appear several times. If a sentence still causes trouble, find its subject and main verb before checking smaller details. You can use a dictionary after you have tried the context. As long as you record the reason for each mistake, your review will be more useful than simply copying answers. 内容4分，三种逻辑从句4分，建议可执行与连贯2分。
