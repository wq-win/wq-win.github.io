# 五上②：饮食、数量与because给理由

> 5年级·上册/阶段；建议分2—4次完成。先独立作答，再核对；能看懂不等于能独立写对。

## 学习目标

- **P24.1** 用What would you like to eat/drink?写清具体需求
- **P24.2** 区分many/much与some/any，读懂份量与食物信息
- **P24.3** 用because连接喜好与理由，避免只列形容词

### 先备与学习方法

前课：en-primary-05a-01。先看例子，再遮住答案写出自己的句子。手写时保留空格；不会的词可先查本课词库，复测时不查。

## 核心讲解

What would you like to eat?问想吃什么；What would you like to drink?问想喝什么。可答I'd like a sandwich and some water.。不要用I am like…替代I would like…。

many apples/sandwiches配可数复数；much water/sugar配不可数名词。a lot of可接两者：a lot of books / a lot of water。some常见于肯定句及礼貌提供食物；any常见于普通疑问句、否定句。规则是常见用法，不是说some永远不能用于问句。

说理由用because＋完整句：I like the soup because it is delicious.。because后若只有delicious，句子不完整；可写because it is delicious。but表达对比：I like sweet food, but I do not eat a lot of it.。

本课只练语言与一般均衡饮食概念，不提供个体诊疗建议，不把单一食物叫作“绝对健康/绝对有害”。题目里的喜好属于人物，不要求学生接受同一口味。

## 例题解析

**例1：**选many/much：There isn't __ milk. milk不可数，选much。

**例2：**连句I like carrots. They are fresh.用because，得I like carrots because they are fresh.。carrots为复数，原因句用they are。

## 原创阅读

On our class picnic, Mei brings two sandwiches and a bottle of water. Ben brings apples and some salad. Mei likes the apples because they are fresh. Ben is thirsty, so Mei shares her water. They do not leave any rubbish in the park.

词义支架：picnic＝野餐；brings＝带来；shares＝分享；rubbish＝垃圾；so＝因此。

2. 填some/any：I have __ apples, but I do not have __ milk.

4. 将I like soup. It is delicious.用because连成一句。

6. 向对方询问想喝什么，写完整问句。

7. 从thirsty/hungry选择：I want some water. I am __.

8. Mei带了什么、各多少？

9. Mei为什么喜欢那些苹果？

## 参考答案与解析

1. many；much。根据名词是否可数选择。

2. some；any。肯定与普通否定的常见用法。

3. two bottles of water。变化在bottles。

4. I like soup because it is delicious.

5. I like apples because they are fresh. 原因句补出主语和be。

6. What would you like to drink?

7. thirsty。想喝水对应口渴。

8. Two sandwiches and a bottle of water.

9. Because they are fresh.

10. Ben口渴；Mei分享水。不要把代词对象倒置。

11. 例：I have two sandwiches. I have a bottle of water and some apples. I like apples because they are fresh. Do not leave rubbish in the park. 四句覆盖全部信息。
