# 六下②：过去的状态与规则动词过去式

> 6年级·下册/阶段；建议分2—4次完成。先独立作答，再核对；能看懂不等于能独立写对。

## 学习目标

- **P39.1** 用was/were描述过去状态并构成否定与疑问
- **P39.2** 用规则动词过去式描述已结束事件，掌握-ed拼写
- **P39.3** 用yesterday/last…等时间线索区别过去与现在

### 先备与学习方法

前课：en-primary-06b-01。先看例子，再遮住答案写出自己的句子。手写时保留空格；不会的词可先查本课词库，复测时不查。

## 核心讲解

过去时间里be变成was/were：I was at home yesterday. / They were happy.。I/he/she/it及单数姓名用was，you/we/they用were。否定was not/wasn't、were not/weren't；疑问Was he…? / Were they…?。不要套现在的am/is/are去写明确结束的过去状态。

规则动词过去式多数加-ed：cleaned、watched；以e结尾加-d：lived；辅音＋y变-ied：studied；play的y前为元音，写played；stop这类短词双写末尾辅音再加-ed：stopped。本课通过文字比较字形，不做词尾发音听辨。

过去肯定句不随主语再加-s：I played. / She played. / They played.。不是She playeds。yesterday、last Sunday、two days ago常提示已结束时间，通常不加on last Sunday或in yesterday。

叙事可按First…Then…After that…组织；先明确何时，再写发生了什么和当时感受。下一课学习实义动词过去句的did问法，不能把所有过去句都随意加was。

## 例题解析

**例1：**They __ at home yesterday. 主语they＋过去，填were。问句为Were they at home yesterday?。

**例2：**“她上周学习了英语”：She studied English last week.。study改studied，不同时加was或-s。

## 原创阅读

Last Saturday, our classroom was untidy after an art activity. Four pupils stayed to help. First, Mei cleaned the desks. Then Ben washed the brushes. Sam and Lin picked up paper from the floor. Our teacher helped us put the pictures on the wall. We were tired, but we were pleased with the clean room. The next morning, the classroom was ready for a new week.

词义支架：untidy＝不整洁的；stayed＝留下；brushes＝刷子；picked up＝捡起；pleased＝满意的；ready＝准备好的。

## 参考答案与解析

1. was；were。过去be与主语搭配。

2. She was not ill yesterday. / She wasn’t ill yesterday.

3. Were they in the classroom?

4. cleaned, lived, studied, played, stopped。

5. She played football yesterday. 过去式不再加-s。

6. We cleaned the room yesterday. 动作过去肯定句不另加were。

7. We were busy two days ago. ago放时间长度后。

8. 在一次美术活动之后。after an art activity提供时间背景，未说明具体是谁怎样把教室弄乱的。

9. 擦桌子、洗刷子、捡纸；老师帮忙挂画。题问四位同学，前三项满足，老师活动不可代替同学活动。

10. Tired but pleased. 不能只写累而漏满意。

11. 示例：Last Sunday, I helped my family at home. First, I cleaned the table and washed some cups. Then I picked up the toys in my room. My sister helped me put the books on the shelf. We were tired, but our home was clean. I was happy because we worked together and finished before lunch. 使用过去动作与was/were，任务项清楚。
