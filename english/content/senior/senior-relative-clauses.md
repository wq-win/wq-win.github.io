# 04 定语从句：限定、补充和准确指代

> 建议位置：高1·上；原创能力递进，不等同于学校实际学期/人教版单元。只训练书面英语。每个编号计一道练习；编号内小项不重复计数。

## 学习目标

- 通过先行词在从句中的成分选择关系词
- 区分限定性与非限定性从句的意义
- 处理介词加关系词、whose及关系词省略
- 在“人物、关系与协作”语境中区分常用词义、搭配并完成新的书面迁移。

## 核心讲解

### 1. 不看中文“的”，看从句缺什么
The researcher **who designed the tool** visited us：从句缺主语，who 指人。The tool **which we tested** worked well：从句缺宾语，可用 which/that，限定性从句中可省。The laboratory **where we tested the tool** is small：从句主谓宾齐全，缺地点状语，可用 where/in which。比较：the laboratory **which we visited**，visited 缺宾语，不能用 where。
### 2. 标点改变信息范围
Students who need help should stay. 只要求“需要帮助的学生”留下。The students, who need help, should stay. 补充说明这群学生都需要帮助。非限定性从句前后用逗号，不能用 that，关系词不能省略。which 还能指前面整件事：The bus was cancelled, which delayed our arrival。
### 3. 介词与所属
The person **to whom** I spoke… 较正式；The person **who/whom/that I spoke to**… 较口语，宾语关系词可省。介词直接提前时不用 that。whose 后接名词，既可指人也可指物：a project whose aim is clear。不能把 which 和 it 同时用于同一缺口：the book which I bought it 错。

### 主题词汇拓展：人物、关系与协作

## 阅读材料



### 主题词汇语境：人物、关系与协作

The apprentice whose model was displayed at the workshop thanked a colleague who had helped her choose materials. Their supervisor, who valued mutual respect, asked them to acknowledge every contribution. A professional carpenter and an amateur designer then discussed the model. They disagreed about one joint, but a respectful exchange helped them reach a compromise. Collaboration did not require identical opinions; it required a clear account of why each person preferred a design.

## 例题解析

**例 1** This is the day ___ we met. → met 不缺宾语，空格表时间，用 when/on which。若改成 This is the day ___ I will never forget，则 forget 缺宾语，用 which/that 或省略。

**例 2** The museum, which reopened last month, has a new gallery. → reopened 的主语由 which 承担；逗号说明重开是附加信息，不能换 that。

**例 3** We met a scientist whose work focuses on water quality. → work 与 scientist 有所属关系，whose 不能换为 who。

## 练习

1. 分别填关系词：a town ___ we visited；a town ___ we stayed for a week。

2. 合并为限定性定语从句：I interviewed a student. Her design won first prize.

3. 改错：The article which I read it yesterday explains the problem.

4. 改写正式结构：The person I sent the application to has replied. 要求把介词提前。

5. 解释意义差别：My brothers who live in Shanghai are teachers. / My brothers, who live in Shanghai, are teachers.

6. 填词并分析指代：The team changed its plan twice, ___ made the preparation more difficult.

7. 【主题词汇·辨义】根据语境区分：①工作同单位的人；②学技能的初学学徒；③没有亲密交情的熟人；④对某领域有专门知识的人。各选本组最贴切词。

8. 【主题词汇·迁移】用两个定语从句介绍一个合作项目：一句用who，一句用whose；至少使用collaborate、contribution、supervisor中的两个词。

## 参考答案与解析

1. which/that（也可省略，visited 的宾语）；where/in which（stayed 不缺宾语，地点状语）。

2. I interviewed a student whose design won first prize. whose design 一起作从句主语，不能写 who her design。

3. 删除 it：The article which I read yesterday explains the problem. which 已充当 read 的宾语，it 重复。

4. The person to whom I sent the application has replied. to 直接提前，需 whom，不能用 that 或省略。

5. 第一句只描述住在上海的那些兄弟，可能另有不住上海的兄弟；第二句把“都住上海”作为对所有兄弟的补充说明，并说他们都是教师。不能据第一句断定一定有其他兄弟，只能说没有排除。

6. which。非限定性 which 指“团队两次改变计划”这件事，而非仅指 plan。从句 made 的主语不能用 what 替代。

7. ①colleague；②apprentice；③acquaintance；④specialist。peer强调同等地位/年龄，不等于同事；relative指亲属，不等于一般“有关的人”。

8. 示例：The students who collaborated on the model shared the results with their supervisor. They thanked the apprentice whose contribution had made the joints stronger. 要点：who作从句主语；whose后接名词；不重复加he/her。
