# 四级读写译八：记叙、描写与说明的不同任务

本课为原创大学书面英语能力课程，不对应统一的人教版大学教材或固定大学年级；不含听力。练习目标只反映本站材料掌握情况，不保证实际考试分数。

<a id="goals"></a>
## 学习目标

1. 按写作目的选择时间顺序、空间顺序或过程顺序。
2. 用具体细节写记叙与描写，用清晰指代和步骤写说明。

3. 在本课主题中理解新增词块的语境用法，并把其中相关词项迁移到新的完整句子。

<a id="core"></a>
## 核心讲解

记叙回答“发生了什么、变化是什么”；描写回答“对象是什么样、为什么这些细节重要”；说明回答“如何运作、为什么发生”。一篇文章可融合体裁，但主任务要清楚。写过程说明时不要花大半篇抒情；写一次经历时不能只有抽象道理。

### 记叙：时态围绕一个叙事时间
Last Friday, I arrived at the library with a list of books for a group project. I had checked the titles but not their locations. After searching the wrong floor for twenty minutes, I asked a librarian for help. She showed me how the shelf numbers worked. I found the first book in minutes and then used the same method for the others. The visit taught me that asking a precise question can save more time than continuing a confused search.

arrived/asked/showed/found为过去叙事；had checked说明到馆前已做的事；末句can表达一般认识，不是随意混时态。

### 描写：选细节而不是堆形容词
The reading room is narrow but carefully arranged. Low shelves run along one wall, leaving a clear path to the windows. Each desk has a small lamp, so students can read without lighting the whole room brightly. Near the entrance, a notice explains where group discussion is allowed. These details make the room feel orderly rather than crowded.

空间线索along one wall、to the windows、near the entrance帮助读者形成位置关系。beautiful, wonderful, amazing连续堆砌不如一个有用途的具体细节。

### 说明：过程要能被执行
To reserve a room, first open the library's booking page and select a date. Next, choose an available time and enter your student number. Check the details before confirming the reservation. If your plans change, use the cancellation link in the confirmation message. Cancelling an unused booking allows another student to use the space.

first/next/before/if对应先后与条件。代词it必须有清楚对象，不能在room、page、date连续出现后说click it而不知点什么。

<a id="worked"></a>
## 例题解析

题目“Describe a place where you study well.”若全文写“Last Monday I went there, then I met a friend…”会偏向事件，缺少环境特征。可保留一句背景，主体选择光线、空间、噪声等与“适合学习”相关的细节。体裁服务任务，不是机械禁止过去时。

<a id="lexical-workshop"></a>
## 词汇工坊

本表补足本课话题的读写词块，含中学承接和大学应用，不将每项都计为大学新词。先遮住中文解释推测例句，再用自己的句子替换人物、时间或对象。**理解义项、掌握搭配、能够迁移**是三项不同任务；下方两道迁移练习检验部分关键词块，不代表全部词项已自动掌握。

| 词项 | 义项与搭配 | 原创语境例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| narrative | 记叙；a personal narrative | A personal narrative needs a clear sequence of events. |
| description | 描写；a concrete description | A concrete description uses observable details. |
| exposition | 说明；a short exposition | The exposition explains how the booking process works. |
| chronological | 按时间的；chronological order | Chronological order helps readers follow the change. |
| spatial | 空间的；spatial organisation | Spatial organisation can guide the reader through a room. |
| turning point | 转折点；a turning point in the story | The request for help became a turning point in the story. |
| setting | 场景；describe the setting | Describe the setting only as far as it matters to the event. |
| specific | 具体的；specific details | Specific details are more informative than repeated praise. |
| vivid | 生动的；a vivid detail | One vivid detail can make a place easier to imagine. |
| sequence marker | 顺序标记；use sequence markers | Sequence markers clarify the order of instructions. |
| procedure step | 操作步骤；a necessary procedure step | Do not omit a necessary procedure step for the sake of brevity. |
| clarity | 清晰；improve clarity | Naming the button can improve clarity. |
| observation | 观察；an observable detail | An observation describes what the writer noticed. |
| retrospect | 回顾；in retrospect | In retrospect, asking for directions would have saved time. |

<a id="practice"></a>
## 练习

先独立完成，再核对答案；开放题须按要点人工评分，不以是否逐字等同范文评分。

### 1

从三段中各找一个组织线索：时间、空间、步骤各一个。

### 2

将I go to the office yesterday and I ask for help改为正确过去叙事。

### 3

将The room is very good扩成两句具体描写，说明至少一个可观察特征及其用途。

### 4

改清指代：Open the page beside the room number and click it to cancel the booking. 若应点击取消按钮，应怎样明确写？

### 5

写60—80词记叙：一次你发现原有方法无效、改变方法的经历。可以写明确标为虚构的学习情景。

### 6

写60—80词过程说明：怎样把一篇读过的短文写成三句摘要。须包括不抄原句和回查原意。

### 7

词汇迁移：译：这段记叙按时间顺序展开，并用一个具体细节说明故事的转折点。

### 8

词汇迁移：译：说明操作步骤时，顺序标记和明确按钮名称都能提高清晰度。

<a id="answers"></a>
## 参考答案与解析

### 1

时间：Last Friday/After searching；空间：along one wall/Near the entrance；步骤：first/Next/before confirming。写等效例子可得分。

### 2

I **went** to the office yesterday and **asked** for help. 两个动作处于同一已结束时间；昨天不适用一般现在时。

### 3

示例：The room has wide desks and adjustable lamps. Students can spread out their notes and direct the light onto the page they are reading. 评分6分：具体特征2、用途关系2、语言2。

### 4

Open the booking page, find your reservation and click the **Cancel** button beside it. it明确回指reservation；不要让click it指向页面或房间号。

### 5

虚构示例：Last week, I tried to memorise a long list of words by reading it repeatedly. The next morning, I could recall very few of them. I then grouped the words by topic and wrote a short sentence with each one. When I tested myself again, I could explain more words without looking at the list. The change reminded me to check what I can use, not merely what looks familiar. 人工10分：事件与转变4、时序2、具体细节2、语言2；不据该个人情景推出方法对所有人必然有效。

### 6

First, read the passage and identify its central point. Next, note two important supporting ideas without copying complete sentences. Put the original aside and write one sentence for each idea in your own words. Finally, compare your summary with the passage. Check that you have kept any important limits and that you have not added a claim the author did not make. 人工10分：步骤4、不抄与回查3、指代连贯1、语言2。


### 7

The narrative develops in chronological order and uses a specific detail to show the turning point. develops与uses平行；turning point不是一般地点。6分：信息3，词块2，句法1。

### 8

Clear sequence markers and explicit button names can improve the clarity of procedure steps. 若使用When explaining…，后面须安排明确的解释者，不能让markers承担解释动作。 6分：信息3，搭配2，句法1。
