<a id="core"></a>
## 核心讲解

讨论自动化与工作，不能只写technology is good/bad。先定义任务：是自动生成排班草稿，还是不经人工审查直接决定员工工作时间？后者涉及额外影响。论点缩小到“哪些任务、什么条件、谁承担检查责任”，才可支持。

反驳有三种合理方式：承认问题但给缓解办法；承认某些情况例外而缩小主张；指出反方前提证据不足。不要给反方扣帽子。**Although automated tools can reduce repetitive work, workers still need a way to question decisions that affect them.** 同时承认效率与申诉需求。

### 原创论证范文
Employers should use automated tools to assist routine scheduling, but they should not treat the resulting timetable as a decision that nobody can question. A useful system must account for workers' circumstances as well as the number of shifts to be filled.

Automation can reduce repetitive administrative work. A tool may quickly identify an unfilled shift or a conflict between two assignments, leaving managers more time to discuss difficult cases with employees. This is a plausible benefit, not a guarantee that every tool will save time in every workplace.

The strongest objection is that rules built into a system may ignore needs that are hard to record. An employee caring for a relative, for example, may be available at times that change from week to week. A timetable can look efficient while placing an unreasonable burden on that person. This concern supports an appeal process and human review, rather than requiring a complete rejection of technology.

The right question is therefore not whether scheduling should be entirely human or entirely automated. It is whether a particular arrangement reduces routine work while keeping responsibility and a meaningful opportunity to correct mistakes.

范文的caregiving情景是说明机制，不冒充调查。最后提出评价标准而非断言“工具必定利大于弊”。

<a id="worked"></a>
## 例题解析

稻草人反驳：People who dislike automation are afraid of progress.
合理反方：An automated schedule may fail to capture changing caring responsibilities.
针对回应：Employees should be able to explain exceptional circumstances and request review. 回应解决信息缺漏，不是说“系统先进所以别担心”。

<a id="answers"></a>
## 参考答案与解析

### 1

Companies could automate selected repetitive tasks if employees can review and correct the results. 条件与范围都明确；不同合理观点可接受。

### 2

快速识别空缺班次和任务冲突，可能减少重复行政工作。may保留工具与情境差异，未提供证明“每处都省时”的证据。

### 3

需要可能变化且不易编码；表面高效的规则可能给特定员工不合理负担。例子不是说所有照护者都无法排班。

### 4

Objection: Some jobs require equipment or face-to-face service at a workplace. Response: Remote work could be an option for suitable tasks rather than a rule applied to every role. 8分：真实相关反方3、回应条件3、语言2。

### 5

An appeal process **can help identify and correct some unfair decisions**, though it must be accessible and taken seriously. 消除all/every保证，保留实际目的。

### 6

可沿讲解范文发展，但须独立选例。另一合理方案是只自动整理材料，不自动决定结果。20分：清楚立场及范围4；机制与具体例子5；公平反方和直接回应5；组织2；准确得体语言4。若未写反方，反方/回应项最高1分；纯“技术万能”而无支持不得用高级词弥补。范文已完整展示可达标结构；合理不同立场同等评分。
