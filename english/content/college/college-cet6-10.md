<a id="core"></a>
## 核心讲解

综合能力要求读懂“系统优化什么”和“使用者需要什么”之间的差异。recommendation在科技语境指推荐内容，不是老师写的推荐信；engagement可能指参与/互动指标，不自动等于获益；personalised不意味着完全理解每个人。

### 原创阅读：A recommendation is a starting point
A learning website recommended articles based on what users had clicked before. Its designers wanted to help readers find material quickly. During a trial, recommended articles received more clicks than articles chosen at random from the same collection. The team regarded this as evidence that previous choices could help predict what would attract attention.

A teacher who used the site welcomed the convenience but raised another question. If a student repeatedly clicked short texts on familiar subjects, would the system keep offering the same kind of material? Interest could make reading easier to begin, but learning sometimes required an unfamiliar topic or a more demanding argument. A recommendation could be attractive without being the most useful next step.

The designers added an explanation beside each suggestion and a separate option labelled “Try something different”. Users could also adjust the level they wanted to practise. These changes made the system's assumptions more visible, but they did not prove that learning had improved. The team would need evidence about what users understood and could later do, not merely what they selected.

The teacher did not argue that recommendations should be removed. Instead, she treated them as starting points for choice. A useful reading plan might combine familiar material that builds confidence with less familiar texts that broaden understanding. Keeping the purpose of learning explicit mattered more than making every selection automatic.

四级阶段能抓“增加点击不等于提高学习”；提高阶段进一步解释指标、目标、可选择性与适当挑战之间的关系，并提出可评估的改进。

<a id="worked"></a>
## 例题解析

不准确推断：The system prevented every student from learning anything new.

原文只是提出重复熟悉主题的风险；后面增加探索选项，没有全体学习结果。应写：**A system based only on past clicks may favour familiar material and therefore may not always support broader learning.** 两处may不是拖泥带水，而是保留证据强度。

<a id="answers"></a>
## 参考答案与解析

### 1

直接显示在这次试验中推荐文获得更多点击；未证明学习理解和后续运用能力改善。没有进一步数据就不能写成绩提升。

### 2

点击可能反映熟悉、短、容易吸引注意；学习有时需要陌生主题和更具挑战的论证。兴趣是起点，不是全部学习效果。

### 3

The website used past clicks to recommend reading, which attracted attention but did not establish better learning. A teacher suggested keeping recommendations as starting points rather than automatic decisions. Explanations, adjustable levels and opportunities to try unfamiliar topics could support choice, while actual understanding would still need to be assessed. 10分：机制2、限制3、回应3、语言2。

### 4

Personalised recommendations can help readers quickly find articles that interest them, but past choices should not be the only basis for selection. Platforms should explain why articles are recommended and preserve opportunities for users to explore different topics. 15分：便利3、非唯一依据4、解释原因3、探索机会3、语言2。可用customised suggestions，但personalised recommendations更贴合科技语域。

### 5

示例：Students should treat reading recommendations as useful suggestions rather than complete learning plans. A recommended article can reduce the time spent searching and make it easier to begin reading, particularly when the subject already interests the learner.

However, convenience is not the only goal. A system that relies on past choices may repeatedly suggest familiar topics or easy texts. Students could miss opportunities to examine a different viewpoint or practise a more demanding structure. They should therefore ask why an article was recommended and whether it matches a current learning goal.

Two habits can help. First, students can combine recommended texts with material from topics they rarely choose. Second, after reading, they can write a brief summary and explain one piece of evidence without looking at the article. This checks more than whether a title attracted attention.

Recommendations do not need to disappear. They become more valuable when students remain responsible for choosing, checking their understanding and gradually widening the range of material they can read.

20分：观点与任务4、便利和限制6、两做法4、组织2、语言4。

### 6

可让学习者隔日不看原文写主旨与一条证据，再用新例子说明同一关系。局限：摘要评分需人工标准，且单次任务不能代表全部能力。8分：贴合理解4、可执行2、限制2。


### 7

A recommendation system should balance novelty and familiarity rather than optimise click-through measures alone. balance A and B；alone限制“只看点击”。6分：信息3，词块2，句法1。

### 8

A delayed summary can check retention, while a new task can test whether learning transfers to an unfamiliar setting. while对比两种用途，不表示因果。6分：信息3，搭配2，句法1。
