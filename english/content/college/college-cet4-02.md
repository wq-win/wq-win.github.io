# 四级读写译二：十五选十的词性与语篇核对

本课为原创大学书面英语能力课程，不对应统一的人教版大学教材或固定大学年级；不含听力。练习目标只反映本站材料掌握情况，不保证实际考试分数。

<a id="goals"></a>
## 学习目标

1. 在15词词库、10空的原创练习中综合词性、搭配与篇章意义选择词项。
2. 解释干扰词为什么不适合具体空格，不把剩余词机械塞入。

3. 在本课主题中理解新增词块的语境用法，并把其中相关词项迁移到新的完整句子。

<a id="core"></a>
## 核心讲解

本课训练词库选择，不是词形变化题：从A—O选词，每词最多使用一次，不改变词形。先通读判话题，再给词库标词性；先做强约束空，再回查弱约束空。最终把10个答案放回全文，确认语法和意思同时成立。官方笔试说明确认选词填空为10题；本课采用15个备选词的原创训练，不复制真题。[CET笔试结构](https://cet.neea.edu.cn/html1/folder/16113/1586-1.htm)

### 原创选词填空：Repairing together
A group of students opened a repair corner in a community centre. Their purpose was to offer (1) ___ advice that visitors could immediately use, rather than lectures on complicated engineering. Anyone could bring a small broken object and discuss whether it was worth repairing. The volunteers hoped to (2) ___ the amount of waste produced when people threw things away too quickly.

At first, many visitors were nervous about touching a tool. They gained (3) ___ when they completed a simple task with help. The volunteers taught basic (4) ___, such as tightening a loose screw and checking a simple connection. Before each activity, they explained the safety rules (5) ___ and made clear which jobs required a trained professional. Nobody was allowed to open equipment that the team could not handle safely.

The project did not encourage people to (6) ___ every broken item with a new one. Instead, it helped them compare the cost of repair with the cost of buying again. Free entry gave residents on low incomes (7) ___ to advice that might otherwise have been difficult to obtain. Some visitors returned with objects that were more (8) ___ to the volunteers, such as desk lamps similar to ones they had already examined.

The repair corner (9) ___ throughout the winter, even after the original student organisers became busy with examinations. Local residents helped to run the sessions. This shared sense of (10) ___ mattered as much as the tools: people began to see the corner as something they could maintain together, not merely a service provided by someone else.

注意：文章是语言学习虚构案例，不是电器维修操作指南。

<a id="worked"></a>
## 例题解析

另一个示例：The team examined the plan ___ before spending money. 选项thorough/thoroughly。修饰examined应选副词thoroughly，意为“彻底地”；形容词thorough适合a thorough examination。确认词性后仍须核对含义；在真正词库中，多个词可能同属一种词性。

<a id="answers"></a>
## 参考答案与解析

### 1

**G practical**。修饰advice；that visitors could immediately use进一步解释“实用的”。financial表示财务的，而后文具体教维修基础，不是财务建议。

### 2

**K reduce**。希望减少丢弃物品产生的垃圾量；to后用原形。

### 3

**B confidence**。gain confidence意为“增强信心”；前句nervous与后句完成小任务构成情绪变化。

### 4

**E skills**。拧紧螺丝、检查简单连接是技能实例，basic skills为自然搭配；不能填damage。

### 5

**N carefully**。修饰explained这一行为，需要方式副词；安全规则要求解释得仔细。urgent是形容词“紧急的”，不能在此作方式副词；不能为了语义将题目给定词形自行改为urgently。

### 6

**C replace**。replace A with B，用B替换A。否定did not encourage说明不主张一坏就买新的，与Instead引出的比较维修成本一致。

### 7

**J access**。give somebody access **to** something，给某人获取……的机会；access在此不可数。

### 8

**L familiar**。be familiar to somebody是“对某人而言熟悉”；相似台灯已检查过，因此熟悉，不是distant。

### 9

**H continued**。全文叙述过去活动，continued throughout the winter表示持续整个冬天；后文居民接力说明项目没有中断。

### 10

**F responsibility**。a sense of responsibility是“责任感”；由居民共同维护而非只依赖他人体现。答案顺序G K B E N C J L H F；剩余A D I M O不使用。

### 11

Beginners participated under supervision and cooperated with neighbours rather than handling tasks that required a professional on their own. 明确合作和专业边界。6分：三项意义3，搭配2，句法1。

### 12

Before buying a replacement, compare whether repair is affordable and whether the item can be reused. before的执行者为隐含you，whether两项平行。6分：信息3，词块2，句法1。
