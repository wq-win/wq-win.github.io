<a id="goals"></a>
## 学习目标

1. 在两篇不同主题文章中分别识别主旨、证据与限制，不混用来源。
2. 对比表面指标与实际目标，完成限时阅读后按错误原因复盘。

3. 在本课主题中理解新增词块的语境用法，并把其中相关词项迁移到新的完整句子。

<a id="core"></a>
## 核心讲解

本站建议首次用12分钟完成两篇阅读及6题，再不限时复盘；这是训练节奏，不是官方单篇规定。先各用一句写主旨，再做题。跨文比较只比较真正共有的关系，不把一篇细节搬进另一篇。

### 原创阅读A：Working less, or hiding the work?
A small design firm tested a four-day working week without reducing salaries. The managers hoped that fewer scheduled hours would encourage staff to organise meetings more carefully. During the trial, teams shortened routine meetings and set aside periods for uninterrupted work. The number of completed client projects remained similar to that of the previous month.

At first sight, this seemed to show that the same output could be produced in less time. Yet an employee survey revealed that some staff were answering messages on their nominal day off. Others said they felt pressure to work faster and take fewer breaks. If work moved outside the recorded schedule, a shorter official week would not necessarily mean a smaller workload. Counting completed projects also said little about how demanding those projects had been.

The firm decided to keep experimenting, but changed what it measured. Staff were asked to record time spent on work outside scheduled hours and to describe the difficulty of their assignments. Managers also discussed expectations about responding to messages. The trial might still offer benefits, but these needed to be assessed against actual working conditions rather than the appearance of a shorter calendar.

### 原创阅读B：Shade that reaches the street
A neighbourhood group proposed planting trees along a busy walking route. Summer afternoons made the pavement uncomfortable, and residents wanted more shade. A sponsor offered funding for a large number of young trees. The proposal attracted attention because the total was easy to announce and sounded ambitious.

A landscape planner supported more planting but questioned the use of tree numbers as the main measure of success. A tree placed where few people walked would not provide the same immediate benefit as one shading a busy crossing. Young trees also needed water and protection while they established roots. Planting many without arranging maintenance could lead to losses that made the impressive initial total misleading.

The group revised the plan around locations, survival and future shade. It identified stretches of pavement with little protection and asked who would maintain each tree. Some planting was delayed until water supplies could be arranged. This reduced the number planted in the first month, but the group regarded delay as preferable to planting trees that were unlikely to survive. It planned to review survival over several seasons rather than declare success on the day of planting.

先独立标记两文的目标、观察和限制；完成练习后再比较它们的关系，不提前假定两篇观点完全相同。

### 1

### 2

A文为什么completed projects相近仍不足以比较生产情况？

### 3

B文规划者是否反对种树本身？用证据回答。

### 4

B文暂缓部分种植的理由是什么？A. The sponsor preferred signs to planting. B. Residents had asked the team to use only mature trees. C. Water supplies for maintenance had to be arranged. D. The route already had enough shade for the trial.

### 5

两文共同支持哪一主张？A. Measures should account for conditions affecting the intended benefits. B. The original indicators became irrelevant once the trials began. C. Shortening the initial schedule was the main solution in both cases. D. Participants’ preferences alone were sufficient to evaluate the plans.

### 6

用60—80个英文词比较两文，明确标记In A / In B，并保留各自具体目标。

### 7

词汇迁移：译：未记录的加班和缩短的休息时段可能隐藏实际工作负担，并影响员工福祉。

### 8

<a id="answers"></a>
## 参考答案与解析

### 1

部分员工休息日仍回消息；部分觉得需加速且减少休息。任两项可得满分，核心是工作可能转移到记录之外或强度上升。

### 2

项目难度未测，相同数量不等于同样工作量；还要纳入非排班工时。不要只说“数字不可信”，数字可真实但信息不完整。

### 3

不反对。supported more planting明确支持；质疑的是只看数量作为成功指标。

### 4

**C**。delayed until water supplies could be arranged；A的资助偏好、B的只要大树、D已有足够遮阴均未得到支持。

### 5

**A**。两文分别把实际工作负担、树木存活与有用遮阴纳入评估。B说原指标完全无关过强；C把B的暂缓误当两文共同方案；D把偏好单独当充分证据。

### 6

In A, a shorter official week may not reduce employees’ actual workload if work continues on days off. In B, planting more trees does not guarantee lasting shade if locations and maintenance are ignored. Both passages suggest checking visible outputs against the intended benefit and revising plans when a simple count hides important conditions. Neither passage recommends abandoning measurement or rejecting the original goal. 12分：A具体3、B具体3、共同关系3、限定和语言3。


### 7

Unrecorded overtime and shorter rest periods may hide the actual workload and affect employee well-being. may表达可能关系，不写已证明疾病。6分：信息3，词块2，句法1。

### 8

After planting saplings along busy pedestrian routes, the team needs longitudinal records of survival rates and shade coverage. after逻辑主语team明确。6分：信息3，搭配2，句法1。
