<a id="goals"></a>
## 学习目标

1. 把全文主旨与某段细节分开，识别过大、过窄和评价失真的标题。
2. 在原创说明文中用限定词和因果关系核对细节题。

3. 在本课主题中理解新增词块的语境用法，并把其中相关词项迁移到新的完整句子。

<a id="core"></a>
## 核心讲解

主旨题要同时覆盖对象、讨论角度和作者判断。标题The History of Technology可能比一篇“图书馆预约系统的小范围试验”大得多；The Colour of the Buttons则只抓无关细节。细节题先定位，再对照主语、时间、数量、条件和程度：only、some、initially、may不是装饰。

### 原创阅读：A quieter way to share a room
At Northfield College, students had long complained that group discussions disturbed people who wanted to study alone. The library had only two large rooms, and neither could be rebuilt before the next academic year. Rather than choosing between complete silence and unrestricted conversation, staff decided to test a schedule. One room would remain quiet throughout the day. The other would allow group work in the afternoon and quiet study in the morning.

Before the trial, some students predicted that changing rules during the day would be confusing. Staff therefore placed clear signs at the entrance and on each table. They also sent a written explanation through the student newsletter. During the first week, volunteers answered questions near the door. Most of the questions concerned when the afternoon session began, rather than whether group work was permitted at all.

After a month, complaints about discussion noise had fallen. However, this did not mean that everyone was satisfied. A group of students taking an evening course reported that they could only meet in the morning, when both rooms required silence. Students working alone also said that the quiet room became crowded after lunch. These comments revealed a weakness that the total number of complaints did not show: a rule could reduce conflict overall while still making access difficult for particular groups.

The library kept the schedule but reserved a smaller office for morning group meetings twice a week. This was not a permanent solution, since staff sometimes needed the office. It was a limited adjustment based on a specific problem. The staff planned another review at the end of the term, when attendance patterns might change with approaching examinations. Their aim was not to find one perfect rule, but to make limited space useful to people with different needs.

这篇文章主线是“有限空间—试行安排—总体改善与分组问题—有限调整”，不是建新馆，也不是证明全日安静最优。

<a id="worked"></a>
## 例题解析

题：The fall in complaints showed that the new schedule met every student's needs. 对还是错？

第三段用However后跟two groups的困难直接限制前句；正确结论只能是投诉减少。**少量投诉指标改善≠所有人的需求满足。** 阅读需要把指标与被推断结论分开。

## 练习

先独立完成，再核对答案；开放题须按要点人工评分，不以是否逐字等同范文评分。

### 1

选最佳标题：A. Extending Library Hours for Evening Students. B. Sharing Limited Study Space More Fairly. C. Designing Clearer Signs for Group Study. D. Reducing Demand for Quiet Study Rooms.

### 2

为什么学校先试时间安排而非立即改建？A. Students had asked staff to replace quiet study with group work. B. The smaller office was already available every morning. C. Staff first needed evidence that discussion noise was a real concern. D. Rebuilding was not possible before the next academic year.

### 3

第一周多数提问针对什么？写出英文证据。

### 4

晨间开会的困难属于哪群人？A. Students on an evening course. B. Students studying alone after lunch. C. Volunteers answering questions at the entrance. D. Students attending the end-of-term review.

### 5

作者如何限制办公室方案的效果？写两个事实。

### 6

用一英文句概括第三段，保留“总体改善但部分群体仍受影响”。

### 7

判断推断：该安排下一学期必然继续不变。能否成立？为什么？

### 8

词汇迁移：译：高峰需求超过了房间容量，因此员工需要更公平地分配空间。

### 9

词汇迁移：译：征求意见可以帮助学校平衡不同需求，并对不便作出相称的回应。

<a id="answers"></a>
## 参考答案与解析

### 1

**B**。覆盖有限空间与不同需要的协调。A把时段内的用途调整换成延长开放时间；C只抓告示这一辅助措施；D把协调需求误写成减少需求。

### 2

**D**。第一段neither could be rebuilt before the next academic year直接支持。A误写学生要求；B把后来的有限办公室安排提前且扩大为每天；C不是文中给出的原因，学生此前早有噪声投诉。

### 3

下午时段何时开始。证据：Most of the questions concerned when the afternoon session began。原文不是“大家不知是否允许讨论”。

### 4

**A**。第三段A group of students taking an evening course…only meet in the morning；上课在晚上不意味着讨论也有晚间时间。

### 5

只每周两次开放小办公室；员工有时仍需使用，非永久方案。写任两项即可，不能扩写成“已经彻底解决”。

### 6

Although complaints decreased, the schedule still limited access for some groups of students. 评分6分：改善2、限制2、准确连接及完整句2。

### 7

不能。最后一段计划学期末再次review，且考试将近时出勤模式可能变化；原文未承诺下一学期安排不变。


### 8

Peak demand exceeded the room's capacity, so staff needed to allocate space more fairly. demand与capacity不是同一概念；exceeded过去时。6分：信息3，搭配2，句法1。

### 9

Consultation can help the college balance different needs and make a proportionate response to the inconvenience. help后两动词平行。6分：信息3，词块2，句法1。
