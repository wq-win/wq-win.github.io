学习目标

  1. 按目的、方法、结果、限制和建议组织短报告，区分观察与建议。

  2. 准确表述比例、百分点和样本范围，不把志愿者样本泛化到全体。

  3. 在本课主题中理解新增词块的语境用法，并把其中相关词项迁移到新的完整句子。

核心讲解

报告不是把数据排成一串。标题让读者知道主题；方法交代谁、何时、怎样回答；结果只报告所得；解释说明可能意义与限制；建议与证据相称。不要用we proved代替小样本描述。

课程虚构数据：午间休息空间调查

40名自愿填写问卷的员工；每人选择最主要需求一项：安静休息20人、方便用餐12人、简短社交8人。人数合计40，比例50%、30%、20%。这不代表公司所有员工的精确需求分布。没有被邀请人数，不能计算response rate。

另两次独立记录显示，一间休息室使用率从40%升至50%。增加10个百分点；相对于原值增加(50−40)/40=25%。二者不能混写“上升10%”。若统计时段或分母不同，比较还需限定。

原创报告示例

Purpose and method This report summarises a voluntary questionnaire about lunchtime spaces. Forty employees selected one main need. The questionnaire did not collect responses from every employee.

Findings Twenty respondents preferred quiet rest, while twelve selected convenient eating facilities and eight chose brief social interaction. Quiet rest was therefore the most common preference in this sample. It was not a preference shared by everyone.

Limitations and recommendation The sample may overrepresent employees who have strong views about the current facilities. The survey also asked about preference, not actual use. A reasonable next step would be to test clearly separated quiet and social areas for two weeks, record use and invite comments from people who did not complete the questionnaire. The findings support a limited trial, not an immediate claim that one design will suit the whole workplace.

健康语境中写quiet rest、workplace comfort是一般环境描述，不据此下医学疗效结论。

例题解析

错误：50% of all workers need medical rest, proving that the workplace causes illness.

原数据是40位志愿者中20人选择安静休息；没有医疗需求、病因或全体员工数据。准确：Half of the forty respondents selected quiet rest as their main lunchtime need.

词汇工坊

本表补足本课话题的读写词块，含中学承接和大学应用，不将每项都计为大学新词。先遮住中文解释推测例句，再用自己的句子替换人物、时间或对象。理解义项、掌握搭配、能够迁移是三项不同任务；下方两道迁移练习检验部分关键词块，不代表全部词项已自动掌握。

词项 义项与搭配 原创语境例句
survey instrument 调查工具；design a survey instrument The survey instrument asked respondents to select one main need.
sampling 抽样；voluntary sampling Voluntary sampling can overrepresent people with strong concerns.
response rate 回应率；calculate the response rate A response rate requires a clearly defined denominator.
frequency table 频数表；read a frequency table The frequency table listed the number choosing each option.
mean 平均数；calculate the mean The mean cannot be calculated without numerical observations.
median 中位数；compare the median The median is determined after ordering the values.
range 范围／极差；report the range A report can use the range to describe variation.
percentage 百分比；express a percentage Express the percentage together with its denominator when useful.
percentage-point change 百分点变化；a percentage-point change A percentage-point change differs from a relative percentage increase.
non-response 未回应；consider non-response Non-response may affect whose views are represented.
self-selection 自我选择；self-selection bias Self-selection bias can occur when participation is voluntary.
tabulate 制成表格；tabulate responses Tabulate responses without changing the original categories.
limitation statement 局限说明；write a limitation statement A limitation statement explains what the findings cannot establish.
recommendation section 建议部分；a proportionate recommendation section The recommendation section should not promise more than the findings support.
