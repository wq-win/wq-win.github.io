学习目标

  1. 把复杂争议拆成条件明确的论点，公平呈现反方并回应其最强理由。

  2. 用机制、例子和限制写出不依赖虚构统计的议论文。

  3. 在本课主题中理解新增词块的语境用法，并把其中相关词项迁移到新的完整句子。

核心讲解

讨论自动化与工作，不能只写technology is good/bad。先定义任务：是自动生成排班草稿，还是不经人工审查直接决定员工工作时间？后者涉及额外影响。论点缩小到“哪些任务、什么条件、谁承担检查责任”，才可支持。

反驳有三种合理方式：承认问题但给缓解办法；承认某些情况例外而缩小主张；指出反方前提证据不足。不要给反方扣帽子。Although automated tools can reduce repetitive work, workers still need a way to question decisions that affect them. 同时承认效率与申诉需求。

原创论证范文

Employers should use automated tools to assist routine scheduling, but they should not treat the resulting timetable as a decision that nobody can question. A useful system must account for workers' circumstances as well as the number of shifts to be filled.

Automation can reduce repetitive administrative work. A tool may quickly identify an unfilled shift or a conflict between two assignments, leaving managers more time to discuss difficult cases with employees. This is a plausible benefit, not a guarantee that every tool will save time in every workplace.

The strongest objection is that rules built into a system may ignore needs that are hard to record. An employee caring for a relative, for example, may be available at times that change from week to week. A timetable can look efficient while placing an unreasonable burden on that person. This concern supports an appeal process and human review, rather than requiring a complete rejection of technology.

The right question is therefore not whether scheduling should be entirely human or entirely automated. It is whether a particular arrangement reduces routine work while keeping responsibility and a meaningful opportunity to correct mistakes.

范文的caregiving情景是说明机制，不冒充调查。最后提出评价标准而非断言“工具必定利大于弊”。

例题解析

稻草人反驳：People who dislike automation are afraid of progress. 合理反方：An automated schedule may fail to capture changing caring responsibilities. 针对回应：Employees should be able to explain exceptional circumstances and request review. 回应解决信息缺漏，不是说“系统先进所以别担心”。

词汇工坊

本表补足本课话题的读写词块，含中学承接和大学应用，不将每项都计为大学新词。先遮住中文解释推测例句，再用自己的句子替换人物、时间或对象。理解义项、掌握搭配、能够迁移是三项不同任务；下方两道迁移练习检验部分关键词块，不代表全部词项已自动掌握。

词项 义项与搭配 原创语境例句
algorithm 算法；a scheduling algorithm A scheduling algorithm applies rules supplied by its designers.
autonomy 自主权；preserve worker autonomy The policy should preserve meaningful worker autonomy.
discretion 酌情判断；human discretion Human discretion can address a case that the rules miss.
transparency 透明度；decision transparency Decision transparency helps workers understand a timetable.
appeal mechanism 申诉机制；an accessible appeal mechanism An appeal mechanism is useful only if workers can use it.
workforce 劳动队伍；a diverse workforce A diverse workforce may have varied scheduling needs.
caregiving 照护；caregiving responsibilities Caregiving responsibilities can change from week to week.
productivity 生产率；measure productivity Productivity should not be inferred from the calendar alone.
intensification 强度增加；work intensification A shorter schedule may coincide with work intensification.
equity 公平；consider equity Consider equity as well as overall efficiency.
displacement 替代造成的转移；job displacement A discussion of job displacement needs more than speculation.
reskilling 技能重训；access to reskilling Access to reskilling may differ between workers.
oversight 监督；human oversight Human oversight requires a real opportunity to change the result.
contest 质疑；contest a decision Employees need a way to contest a decision that affects them.

需要可能变化且不易编码；表面高效的规则可能给特定员工不合理负担。例子不是说所有照护者都无法排班。
