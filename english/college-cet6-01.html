学习目标

  1. 还原否定倒装、强调结构、比较省略及嵌套从句，不改变逻辑范围。

  2. 在复杂句中保留推测、限制和条件，写出准确中文释义。

  3. 在本课主题中理解新增词块的语境用法，并把其中相关词项迁移到新的完整句子。

核心讲解

从四级向六级提高，不是把每个短词换成长词，而是增加信息密度、抽象关系与论证限制。本课程在这一层加入嵌套结构、谨慎论断和多个解释的比较；不声称真实考试每句必然比四级长。

Only after the team checked the records did it realise that the apparent increase reflected a change in reporting. Only+状语放句首，主句部分倒装did it realise；that从句仍为陈述语序。还原The team realised…only after…。“直到核对后才意识到”，不能漏only。

Not until the costs were included did the proposal appear less attractive. 还原The proposal did not appear less attractive until the costs were included。这里did后appear原形；不要写did appeared。倒装发生在主句，不在until从句。

What matters is not whether the tool is new, but whether it helps users complete a task. What引导主语从句，相当于the thing that matters；两个whether从句是并列的表语内容。not…but…否定“新不新是关键”，肯定“是否有助完成任务才关键”。

The policy, if applied without exceptions, could disadvantage people whose schedules are irregular. if applied=if it is applied；省略前提是主语可从policy恢复。could是可能，不是已证实。whose schedules说明“那些人的时间表”。

The second survey included fewer first-year students than the first did. did代included，比较的是两次survey包括的一年级学生人数，不是总学生人数。as much a question of access as of motivation表示同样涉及可达性与动机，并非二选一。

处理密集名词短语也要恢复关系：the effect of reduced staffing on evening access =减少人手对晚间可使用情况的影响。先找effect中心词，再分清of原因和on受影响对象。

例题解析

Had the researchers relied only on completed forms, they would have overlooked people who could not use the online system.

Had置首是不带if的过去反事实条件：If the researchers had relied…。主句would have overlooked表示本会漏掉。译：“研究者如果当时只依赖已填完的表格，就会忽略那些无法使用在线系统的人。”它不是在陈述“研究者确实只依赖表格”；条件形式告诉我们不要把假设误读为事实。

词汇工坊

本表补足本课话题的读写词块，含中学承接和大学应用，不将每项都计为大学新词。先遮住中文解释推测例句，再用自己的句子替换人物、时间或对象。理解义项、掌握搭配、能够迁移是三项不同任务；下方两道迁移练习检验部分关键词块，不代表全部词项已自动掌握。

词项 义项与搭配 原创语境例句
inversion 倒装；partial inversion Only introduces partial inversion in this sentence.
ellipsis 省略；recover ellipsis Recover the ellipsis before comparing the two clauses.
nesting 嵌套；clause nesting Clause nesting makes the relationship harder to see.
counterfactual 反事实的；a counterfactual condition The counterfactual condition describes what did not happen.
emphasis 强调；place emphasis on The structure places emphasis on the condition.
restriction of scope 范围限定；a restriction of scope Not all creates a restriction of scope rather than total rejection.
syntactic 句法的；syntactic analysis Syntactic analysis separates the main claim from its modifiers.
nominalisation 名词化；avoid excessive nominalisation Excessive nominalisation can hide who performed an action.
recover 还原；recover the omitted verb Recover the omitted verb from the preceding clause.
concurrent 同时的；concurrent changes Concurrent changes make a causal explanation harder to isolate.
subsequent 随后的；subsequent analysis Subsequent analysis revealed a reporting difference.
preceding 前面的；the preceding clause The pronoun refers to a claim in the preceding clause.
embedded assumption 隐含嵌入假设；an embedded assumption The argument contains an embedded assumption about equal access.
structural ambiguity 结构歧义；resolve structural ambiguity Context can help resolve structural ambiguity.

练习

先独立完成，再核对答案；开放题须按要点人工评分，不以是否逐字等同范文评分。

1

还原Only when the sample was enlarged did the pattern become clear为正常语序，并翻译only的限制。

2

翻译：What remains unclear is whether the improvement will last after the trial ends.

3

补出省略成分：The guide, if updated regularly, could remain useful for several years.

4

翻译：Not all benefits that are easy to measure are the most important ones.

5

写出Had the team consulted users, it would have noticed the barrier earlier的if版本，并说明是否断言团队当时咨询过用户。

6

解释比较：The revision raises as many questions as it answers. 是否表示只回答不提问？

7

词汇迁移：译：句法分析帮助我们还原省略的动词，并区分主张与范围限定。

8

词汇迁移：译：这个反事实条件不是已经发生的事实；后续分析必须保留这种区别。

参考答案与解析 · 完成练习后展开

参考答案与解析

1

The pattern became clear only when the sample was enlarged. “只有在样本扩大后，模式才变得清楚。”不是样本扩大前就清楚。

2

“仍不清楚的是，试行结束后这种改善是否会持续。”whether为间接疑问；will last属于待确认内容，不是作者保证。

3

if it is updated regularly。it=the guide，含被动。整句“指南如果定期更新，可能多年保持实用”；不是说已定期更新。

4

“并非所有容易衡量的益处都是最重要的益处。”not all不同于none；定语从句限制benefits的范围。

5

If the team had consulted users, it would have noticed the barrier earlier. 过去反事实语境通常表示当时并未这样做；不能当成“已咨询并发现”。

6

表示这次修订提出的问题与解答的问题一样多，通常暗含仍有不少待解之处。as many…as…是数量比较，不是“没有回答任何问题”。

7

Syntactic analysis helps us recover the omitted verb and distinguish the claim from its restriction of scope. help后动词并列，distinguish A from B。6分：信息3，搭配2，句法1。

8

The counterfactual condition does not describe an event that actually happened; subsequent analysis must preserve this distinction. 分号连接，actually happened受否定describe辖制。6分：信息3，词块2，句法1。
