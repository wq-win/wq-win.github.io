核心讲解

本卷是书面英语阅读训练，不提供个人医疗建议。文中为原创研究情境，不应据此诊断疾病或改变治疗。建议60分钟，总分90：1—6各5分，7—8各10分，9为15分，10为25分。多空题均分；阅读解释按对应证据与推理均分；两项写作按量规。

材料强调“观察关联≠已证明原因”，同时不能反向推成“关联毫无价值”。复习定位：词义/语域→第26课；长句→第27课；综合阅读→第31课；编辑→第34课。

阅读材料

Original assessment reading: A Break in the Working Day

A recent workplace questionnaire asked participants how often they took short breaks and how comfortable they felt at the end of the day. People who reported regular breaks also tended to report greater comfort. A headline summarised the result as “Breaks Solve the Problem”.

The researchers were more cautious. Their survey recorded an association, not the result of assigning people to different schedules. Participants worked in different settings, and the study relied on their descriptions rather than direct observation. Desk arrangements, workload and previous discomfort might influence both how often someone paused and how that person felt. It was also possible that some people began taking breaks because they were already uncomfortable.

These limits did not make the survey useless. It could help identify questions for a more focused study, such as how different patterns of activity relate to comfort under comparable working conditions. The researchers warned that habits which appear together in a survey may not stand in a simple cause-and-effect relationship. The result was a starting point for investigation, not a prescription for every individual.

例题解析

示范 People who reported regular breaks also reported less discomfort. 直接支持两种自报现象相关；不自动支持 breaks cured disease。观察可以提出值得进一步研究的问题，但需更严格设计检验原因。

练习

  1. What does the study directly report? A. A measured improvement in the same workers over several years. B. A direct observation of how often workers moved. C. An association between reported breaks and reported comfort. D. A comparison of two assigned break schedules.

  2. 列出两种可能影响结果、未被单纯问卷排除的因素。

  3. “The result is a starting point, not a prescription.” 中prescription在本文如何理解？

  4. 作者为什么仍认为研究有价值？用一条准确证据回答。

  5. 填词：People who ___ (report) taking breaks also said they felt more comfortable. The survey was completed last month.

  6. 改错：Not all of the participants was office workers, which means none of them worked in an office.

  7. 分析并译出：The researchers warned that habits which appear together in a survey may not stand in a simple cause-and-effect relationship.

  8. 用50—65词作摘要，保留关联、限制、进一步研究三个信息点。

  9. 写90—110词解释段：为什么阅读健康新闻时要看研究如何进行，而不只看标题？可引用本篇，但不得给个人医疗方案。

  10. 阅读微故事并写约120词单段续写：Sam答应把借来的旧相册按顺序整理，却把两张无日期照片的位置弄混。主人Ms Chen回来时，他正想悄悄凭感觉放回；此时她问：“What did you learn from the pictures?” 从Sam admitted his uncertainty. 开始，保持第三人称过去时；通过询问与照片细节合理解决或标注不确定，不让他突然知道真实日期。

参考答案与解析 · 完成练习后展开

参考答案与解析

  1. C。研究直接记录两个自报指标的关联；未分配日程做干预，未多年追踪同一人改善，也不是现场直接观察。

  2. 工作种类、原有舒适度/健康状况、椅子或桌面环境、压力、是否因不适而更常休息等。任选两项，须说明它们可同时影响休息行为与感受，不能凭问卷完全区分。

  3. 比喻可直接照做的统一建议/处方。作者说结果只是进一步研究起点，不是给所有人的个性化健康指令。

  4. 可帮助研究者提出更具体问题、设计比较不同日程/记录实际行为的进一步研究；价值在生成可检验问题，不是已经证明治疗效果。

  5. reported。last month限定调查为过去，reported为从句有限谓语；主句said也在过去。

  6. Not all of the participants were office workers, which means that some worked in other settings. participants复数用were；not all仅否定全部，不能推出none。要更严格可写 The sentence does not tell us exactly how many worked in offices。

  7. 主干researchers warned；that…为宾语从句；habits为从句主语，which appear together…修饰habits；may not…表达不确定否定。译：研究者提醒，在调查中一同出现的习惯，未必存在简单的因果关系。

  8. 示例：The survey found that people reporting regular breaks also reported greater comfort. However, it relied on self-reports and did not rule out differences in work, surroundings or previous discomfort. The findings can guide more focused research, but they do not establish that breaks caused the reported comfort or provide a personal treatment plan. 量规10：关联3；限制3；进一步研究2；语言2。

  9. 示例：A health headline may present a simple answer even when the study behind it asks a narrower question. Readers should check who participated, what was measured and whether the researchers observed habits or tested an intervention. In the break survey, comfort and behaviour were both self-reported, so the association did not establish a single cause. Details about other factors and the length of the study also affect how widely a result can apply. Reading the method therefore helps us distinguish a useful clue from a proven explanation, without assuming that every limited study is worthless. 量规15：研究方法重要性3；具体核查要素5；文本例证3；谨慎结论2；语言2。

  10. 示例：Sam admitted his uncertainty. He placed the two photographs side by side and explained that he could not tell which came first. Ms Chen leaned closer. In one picture, she noticed a young tree beside the house; in the other, the same tree reached the window. “That helps,” she said, “but I still don’t remember the exact years.” Sam wrote a small note recording what they could infer and what remained unknown. Instead of forcing the pictures into numbered spaces, he put them in a separate sleeve with the note. When Ms Chen thanked him for being careful, he realised that organising a memory did not mean pretending to know every part of it. 量规25：承认不确定及人物一致5；合理证据/非魔法解决7；首句时态和视角4；叙事衔接4；语言5。

订正与迁移

先遮住答案独立完成，再为每处错误记录“语境线索—适用规则—我的新例句”。隔一天换一个主题复做，不以背熟本页答案代替迁移。完整篇章写作按题后量规人工核查，短句产出按答案中的结构与信息要点自查；不把示例当成唯一答案。
