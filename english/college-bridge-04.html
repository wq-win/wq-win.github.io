学习目标

  1. 按搭配和语境区分常见词的多个义项及近义词。

  2. 把口语化或夸大的句子改成得体、准确的书面表达。

  3. 在本课主题中理解新增词块的语境用法，并把其中相关词项迁移到新的完整句子。

核心讲解

会背汉语意思不等于会用。address可作“地址”，也可作动词“处理”：The report addresses the shortage of study spaces；issue可作“议题”，issue a notice则是“发布通知”；account可指账户，account for意为“解释／占比例”；charge a fee是收费，be in charge of是负责。阅读时先问词性，再问搭配，最后看篇章话题。

近义词不是任意替换

  • rise是不及物：Prices rose. raise通常及物：The shop raised its prices. 过去式分别rose、raised。
  • affect常为动词影响：Noise affects concentration. effect常为名词结果：Noise has an effect on concentration。本课不把少见动词effect混进基础选择题。
  • economic与经济相关：economic growth；economical节省的：an economical heating system。
  • sensible明智的；sensitive敏感的。a sensible plan与sensitive personal information不能互换。
  • provide somebody with something / provide something for somebody；不能照中文写provide somebody something用于本课程的规范目标句。

语域与证据强度

给朋友留言可写Thanks for the info；给不熟悉的教师写邮件，可写Thank you for the information。正式不等于把简单词换成最生僻的词：use往往比utilise更直接；buy在普通说明中完全正确。

避免无证据绝对化：The system is perfect and will solve every problem应改为The system may reduce waiting times, although its maintenance costs need further review。修改不是“变长”，而是补出作用范围和限制。词语very good可在具体语境中改为clear、accurate、reliable，而非一律替换为excellent。

例题解析

原句：This cheap plan will definitely fix all the students' problems.

cheap可能只表示便宜，也可能暗含质量低；若证据是“费用低”，可用low-cost。all the students' problems范围过大，先具体化到study-space shortage。若尚未实施，will definitely过强。改为：This low-cost plan could help address the shortage of study spaces. 没有删除对计划的支持，只把支持压到证据允许的程度。

本课词汇与搭配

下列为本课实际讲解的词项；词形变化及同一词的不同义项不等于新增独立词汇量。

词项 本课义项及用法
address v. 处理／回应；address a problem
issue n. 议题；v. 发布，issue a notice
account for 解释；占比例，account for half of the total
raise v. 提高；raise prices，过去式raised
economic adj. 经济的；economic development
economical adj. 节省的；economical to run
sensitive adj. 敏感的；sensitive information
access n. 使用／获取机会；access to information

词汇工坊

本表补足本课话题的读写词块，含中学承接和大学应用，不将每项都计为大学新词。先遮住中文解释推测例句，再用自己的句子替换人物、时间或对象。理解义项、掌握搭配、能够迁移是三项不同任务；下方两道迁移练习检验部分关键词块，不代表全部词项已自动掌握。

词项 义项与搭配 原创语境例句
register 语域；formal register A formal register does not require obscure words.
colloquial 口语化的；a colloquial expression The colloquial expression suits a friendly message.
neutral 中性的；neutral wording Neutral wording avoids an accusation without evidence.
polite 礼貌的；a polite request A polite request can still be direct.
appropriate 合适的；appropriate for The phrase is appropriate for an academic report.
connotation 附加含义；a negative connotation Cheap can have a negative connotation in some contexts.
literal 字面的；a literal meaning The literal meaning does not fit this sentence.
idiomatic 地道自然的；idiomatic English A clear collocation helps produce idiomatic English.
exaggerate 夸大；exaggerate a benefit Do not exaggerate a benefit that has not been measured.
understate 轻描淡写；understate a difficulty The phrase may understate the difficulty faced by users.
respectful 尊重人的；a respectful tone A respectful tone separates disagreement from personal criticism.
nuance 细微差别；a difference in nuance The two words share a broad meaning but differ in nuance.
assertion 断言；an unsupported assertion An unsupported assertion becomes no stronger when repeated.
word choice 选词；careful word choice Careful word choice keeps the claim within the evidence.

练习

先独立完成，再核对答案；开放题须按要点人工评分，不以是否逐字等同范文评分。

1

选rise/raise并用过去式：The college ___ the fee, so the total cost ___.

2

选economic/economical：The report discusses ___ development; the new lamp is more ___ to run.

3

解释address：The guide addresses the needs of first-year students. 不是“地址”的哪个义项？

4

用provide改写：The library gives students free access to journals. 写两种结构。

5

改正式邮件句：Hey teacher, send me the stuff ASAP. 保留“请求课程材料”，不保留无理由的催促。

6

改准确表达：Our survey proves that everybody hates online courses. 已知只调查本班20名志愿者，12人表示更喜欢线下课。

7

填affect/effect：The change may ___ attendance, but its exact ___ is unclear.

8

词汇迁移：译：正式语域需要合适的选词和尊重人的语气，而不一定需要生僻词。

9

词汇迁移：译：中性措辞不应夸大好处，也不应淡化使用者遇到的困难。

参考答案与解析 · 完成练习后展开

参考答案与解析

1

raised; rose。第一空有宾语the fee，第二空主语cost自行上升；两句为过去时。

2

economic; economical。前者讨论经济发展，后者评价运行是否节省。

3

address是及物动词“回应／处理／顾及”；句意“指南针对一年级学生的需求”。读为“给需求写地址”不合语义。

4

The library provides students with free access to journals. / The library provides free access to journals for students. access通常不可数，常搭配access to。

5

Dear Professor Chen, Could you please send me the course materials when convenient? Thank you for your help. 若并非教授，称谓改为对方已知合适称谓；“ASAP”不能在没有紧急理由时作为礼貌请求的默认结尾。

6

In a survey of 20 volunteers from our class, 12 said they preferred in-person courses. 这只报告样本及偏好，不等于“讨厌网课”，也不能推到everybody或因果prove。

7

affect; effect。may后需动词原形；its exact后需名词。

8

A formal register requires appropriate word choice and a respectful tone, not necessarily obscure words. not necessarily不等于绝不；三个名词块平行。6分：三项意义3，搭配2，句法1。

9

Neutral wording should neither exaggerate the benefits nor understate the difficulties users face. neither…nor后动词原形平行。6分：双方限制3，搭配2，句法1。
